About EasyFlux

EasyFlux Consulting is a healthcare management consultancy. We work with clinics, hospitals, healthcare providers, and public health bodies to find what's slowing them down, bring in tools and systems that fit, and improve the way care reaches patients.

We bring together international consulting experience and a close understanding of how healthcare actually runs in emerging markets. We know the constraints, we know what's possible, and we know what tends to work on the ground.

Our team brings together medical professionals, IT specialists, data managers, and people who have spent years leading change in healthcare settings. We are proficient in Waterfall, Agile, and Wagile delivery, and we choose the approach that fits the work.

What We Do

We focus on three areas. Within each, we offer specific pieces of work with clear timelines and clear outcomes, so you know what you're getting before we start.

1

Digital Health Transformation

Helping healthcare organisations move from paper-based and fragmented operations toward connected, digitally enabled services that improve patient access and care coordination.

  • Digital Health Strategy Sprint. A four-week piece of work that gives you a clear picture of where you are, where you want to get to, and the steps to take you there.
  • Digital Health Readiness Assessment. A structured look at how ready your people, processes, data, and technology are for digital change.
  • Automation and Workflow Improvement. Spotting and putting in place the small changes that take repetitive admin work off your team's plate.
  • Digital Health Technology Advice. Honest, vendor-neutral guidance on choosing electronic health records, patient portals, telehealth, and the systems that sit behind them.
2

Healthcare Operations and Process Improvement

Strengthening the everyday systems that decide whether a healthcare organisation runs well or runs into the ground.

  • Service Optimisation Reviews. Looking at how patients move through your service, where things get stuck, and what to change.
  • Data Governance and Policy Development. Setting up the rules and routines that make your health data reliable, useful, and trusted.
  • Innovation Management Advice. Helping leadership teams build the habits and structures to spot good ideas, test them, and grow the ones that work.
  • Strategic Advice. Supporting senior leaders and boards on direction, change, and the bigger calls.
3

Capability Building and Training

Building the skills inside your organisation, so the changes we make together carry on after we're gone.

  • Strategic Innovation Mastery Programme. A four-week programme that helps participants spot innovation opportunities and turn them into real services or businesses. We've delivered this to professional groups before.
  • Team Digital Readiness Training. A two-week course that prepares healthcare staff to use digital tools confidently in their day-to-day work.
  • Tailored Training. Custom programmes for clinical, administrative, and leadership teams, built around what your organisation actually needs.

How We Work

Clearly Scoped Engagements

We work in clearly scoped engagements with defined deliverables, fixed timelines, and transparent fees, rather than open-ended consulting contracts.

Training and Strategy Sprints

Most of our work falls between two-week training courses and four-week strategy sprints. Where you need longer support to see things through, we can stay with you for that too. We work remotely, on-site, or a mix of both, whichever suits the work.

Tailored to Your Context

Whether you're a single clinic, a hospital group, a ministry, or a development partner, we shape what we do around your size and your priorities.
