Brain Health Guide

Evidence-based strategies to protect cognitive function

🏃 Physical Activity
The most powerful modifiable risk factor
🚶
150 Min/Week
Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity weekly. Even brisk walking counts.
💪
Strength Training
Resistance exercise 2×/week improves brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) levels.
🧘
Mind-Body Exercise
Yoga, tai chi, and dance combine physical and cognitive benefits.
🥦 Diet & Nutrition
The MIND diet shows up to 53% reduced Alzheimer's risk
🫒
Mediterranean Diet
Olive oil, fish, vegetables, legumes, and whole grains are strongly neuroprotective.
🫐
Berries & Leafy Greens
Rich in flavonoids and antioxidants. Aim for leafy greens daily, berries twice weekly.
🚫
Limit Processed Foods
Ultra-processed food, red meat, and saturated fats are linked to increased cognitive decline.
😴 Sleep
Deep sleep clears amyloid plaque from the brain
⏰
7–9 Hours
Both too little and too much sleep are associated with faster cognitive decline.
🌙
Sleep Consistency
Regular sleep/wake times regulate circadian rhythm and improve glymphatic clearance.
📱
Screen-Free Bedtime
Stop screens 1 hour before bed. Blue light suppresses melatonin and disrupts deep sleep.
🧩 Cognitive Engagement
Build cognitive reserve to delay symptom onset
📚
Lifelong Learning
Learning new skills, languages, or instruments creates new neural pathways at any age.
♟️
Strategic Games
Chess, crosswords, puzzles, and strategy games challenge working memory and processing.
✍️
Journaling
Writing engages language, memory, and executive function simultaneously.
👥 Social Connection
Social isolation doubles dementia risk
🤝
Regular Social Contact
Aim for meaningful social interactions daily — in-person has stronger benefits than digital.
🎭
Community Groups
Clubs, volunteering, and group activities combine social and cognitive stimulation.
🩺 Medical Management
Controlling vascular risk factors is crucial
💊
Blood pressure control
Hypertension in midlife is one of the strongest modifiable risk factors for dementia.
🩸
Manage diabetes & cholesterol
Poor glycemic control damages small blood vessels supplying the brain.
👂
Address hearing loss
Untreated hearing loss is the single largest modifiable risk factor. Use hearing aids.
🧠
Treat depression
Depression raises dementia risk by ~65%. Seek treatment — therapy, medication, or both.
